# Triad non-claims and assurance boundary

Status: **DOCUMENTATION / UNVERIFIED**. This page imposes constraints on assertions; it is not evidence of enforcement.

The bounded Triad prototype **does not establish**:
- A complete AGI governance system or general solution to alignment.
- A production safety guarantee or suitability for high-stakes deployment.
- A distributed constitutional runtime, consensus mechanism, or multi-writer safety.
- An external audit, independent replication, or independently controlled witness.
- Hardware safety, secure key custody, or institutional legitimacy.
- Certification, regulatory approval, or E4/W1/O1 advancement.

Local or origin-controlled tests, including CI and mutation tests, do not by themselves establish independent reproduction. A signed receipt proves only what its trust chain, measured bytes, runtime, and verifier actually bind; signing does not prove that an actor had legitimate authority.

## Claim discipline

Use the canonical vocabulary in [EVIDENCE_LABELS.md](EVIDENCE_LABELS.md). Describe a proposed design as `HYPOTHESIS`, untested implementation as `UNVERIFIED`, and a third-party assertion as `REPORTED`. Only use `VERIFIED-REPO` for a narrowly scoped assertion with checked-in implementation and repeatable commands or reviewable receipts. Reproduction failures require a downgrade, not a rewritten narrative.

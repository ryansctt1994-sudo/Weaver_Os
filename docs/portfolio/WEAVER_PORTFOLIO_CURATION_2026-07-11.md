## Promotion standard

A project may move upward only when the promotion record contains:

1. Repository and immutable commit pin.
2. Source bytes or reproducible checkout path.
3. Exact environment/toolchain.
4. Exact build/test/replay commands.
5. Fresh output or transcript.
6. SHA-256 or stronger binding for artifacts and receipts.
7. Negative controls or adversarial tests where relevant.
8. Witness identity and independence level.
9. Explicit non-claims and unresolved limitations.

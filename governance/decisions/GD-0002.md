## What is accepted

Lumen-Nexus `governance/adr/ADR-0006-receipt-ledger.md`, as committed at
`14fe8fe0179da7133f6400178d78f7603e678bb7`, SHA-256
`e97f3867decda624dd41b375d0993b0e3afe20a3aed4e63e426ea707739b6934`:

> One state-owning `ReceiptLedger` boundary responsible for canonical event admission,
> predecessor selection, monotonic sequence assignment, atomic append, chain-head persistence,
> receipt materialization after successful commit, and read-only snapshots for verifiers.
> Callers provide logical event content. They do not provide or mutate predecessor hashes,
> sequence values, ledger heads, or committed receipt objects.

The ADR's seven required invariants are adopted as stated there. In this repository they are
implemented by `weaver_runtime.ledger` and `weaver_verifier.ledger`
(see `governance/specs/WX-RECEIPT-LEDGER-1.md`, which maps each one to tests).

## Scope

This decision accepts ADR-0006 **for `Weaver_Os`** (the unified system). The copy in Lumen-Nexus
is unchanged and still reads PROPOSED there, under that repository's own seal and governance.
When Lumen-Nexus is merged in, its record should cite GD-0002 rather than be edited to look as
if it had been accepted there.
